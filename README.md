One single-line joke about a meeting that should have been an email, told from a
fresh angle: the email didn't get replaced by the meeting — it got promoted into
one, complete with a calendar invite, twelve salaries on the payroll, and the
final indignity of reading itself aloud to the very people who wrote it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) writes the one-line entry
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after checking the line
is 1–200 non-blank characters.
